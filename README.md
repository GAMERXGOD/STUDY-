# 🌙 Luna Pomodoro Timer

A beautiful, minimal, and distraction-free **Pomodoro timer** designed to help you focus, work consistently, and take intentional breaks.

Luna combines a clean interface with a simple productivity workflow so you can stay in the zone without unnecessary distractions.

## ✨ Features

* 🍅 **Pomodoro Timer** — Work in focused sessions followed by short breaks.
* ⏱️ **Customizable Sessions** — Adjust work and break durations to fit your workflow.
* 🔔 **Session Notifications** — Know when it's time to work or take a break.
* 🎯 **Focus-Friendly UI** — Minimal interface designed to keep distractions away.
* 🌙 **Luna Theme** — Clean, modern aesthetics with a dark, calming design.
* 📱 **Responsive Design** — Works across desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
* ⚡ **Lightweight & Fast** — No unnecessary complexity.
* 🔄 **Start / Pause / Reset** — Full control over your current session.
* 📊 **Session Tracking** — Keep track of your completed focus sessions.

## 🧠 What is the Pomodoro Technique?

The **Pomodoro Technique** is a time-management method where you divide your work into focused intervals separated by short breaks.

A typical cycle looks like:

```text
🍅 Focus       → 25 minutes
☕ Short Break → 5 minutes
🍅 Focus       → 25 minutes
☕ Short Break → 5 minutes
🍅 Focus       → 25 minutes
☕ Short Break → 5 minutes
🍅 Focus       → 25 minutes
🌿 Long Break  → 15–30 minutes
```

The idea is simple:

> **Focus deeply. Rest intentionally. Repeat.**

## 🎨 Design Philosophy

Luna is built around three principles:

### 🌙 Calm

The interface should feel relaxing rather than overwhelming.

### 🎯 Focused

Only the information needed during a focus session should demand your attention.

### ✨ Simple

Productivity shouldn't require learning a complicated productivity system.
